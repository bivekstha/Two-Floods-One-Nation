## 📖 About the Project

The **Nepal Flood Awareness Project** is an academic research and
social-awareness initiative focused on understanding the impact of
floods in Nepal, with particular attention to the regions of **Rasuwa
and Gorkha**.

This project explores the environmental, social, and economic
consequences of flooding through research, articles, visual
storytelling, and awareness publications.

It aims to bring attention to affected communities, highlight their
experiences, and promote awareness about disaster preparedness and
climate vulnerability.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

-   Raise awareness about flood disasters in Nepal.
-   Explore the environmental and social consequences of flooding.
-   Highlight the challenges faced by affected communities.
-   Present research findings through visual storytelling.
-   Encourage disaster preparedness and community awareness.
-   Communicate important information through creative publications.

## 📚 Project Publications

### 1. Digital E-Magazine

An editorial-style digital magazine featuring research, articles,
interviews, photographs, statistics, and experiences related to flooding
in Nepal.

**Key contents:** - Editorial and introduction - Research-based feature
articles - Flood impact analysis - Community experiences and case
studies - Statistics and visual representations - Interviews and
awareness content - Research methodology and references

**File:** `E-Magazine/Nepal-Flood-E-Magazine.pdf`

### 2. Awareness Brochure

A visual awareness brochure designed to communicate essential
information about Nepal's flood situation in a concise and engaging
format.

**Key contents:** - Overview of the flood situation - Affected regions:
Rasuwa and Gorkha - Environmental and social impacts - Disaster
preparedness and safety awareness - Important information and references

**File:** `Brochure/Nepal-Flood-Brochure.pdf`

## 🌍 Why This Project Matters

Natural disasters affect more than infrastructure. They impact families,
livelihoods, education, transportation, and entire communities.

Through this project, the goal is to communicate the human side of
natural disasters while encouraging awareness, research, and
preparedness.
